What is ReelVotes?

ReelVotes is a community movie club where people suggest films, vote on what screens next, and then meet up for a shared theater experience.

  • Vote windows and screening drops are announced by email first.
  • You’ll get direct links when voting opens and when tickets go live.
  • No spam — just movie-night updates and relevant announcements.
