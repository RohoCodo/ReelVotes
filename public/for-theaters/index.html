How ReelVotes works with your theater

We run a structured process designed to reduce risk and ensure demand before a screening is scheduled:

  1. Community Vote (5–7 days)
    Audiences vote on upcoming films from a curated selection.
  2. Licensing & Confirmation (a few days)
    We secure screening rights for the winning title. Your theater always has final approval before anything is scheduled.
  3. Test Ticket Sales Period
    We open early ticket sales to validate demand. A minimum threshold is set by your theater to ensure the screening is financially viable.
  4. Scheduling the Screening
    If the sales threshold is met, we schedule the screening for a date you select in advance (typically a few weeks out).

Marketing responsibilities

  • We promote the voting process and drive audience participation.
  • We also help promote the test ticket sales period to help reach your minimum threshold.
  • Your theater continues to market the screening through your normal promotional channels.

How movie selections are determined

  • Community-submitted suggestions for themed or open votes.
  • Our “Reel Success” tool, which selects films based on performance data at similar theaters.
  • Curated lists based on what has performed well at comparable venues.
  • Full theater control — you can provide or approve all film selections.

Control & approvals

Your theater retains full control over:

  • Which films are eligible for screening.
  • Final approval of any scheduled movie night.
  • What dates and times screenings occur.
  • Control over ticket pricing.

All screenings are subject to licensing availability and approval.
