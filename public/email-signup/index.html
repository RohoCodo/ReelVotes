Frequently asked questions

How often will I actually receive emails?+

There's no fixed schedule. Emails go out when something meaningful happens: a new campaign gains momentum, a vote winner is confirmed, or tickets go live.

Will my email address be shared with theaters or other companies?+

No. Your email is used only for ReelVotes voting and ticket alerts.

Can I get alerts for more than one theater at once?+

Yes. One signup can cover updates across participating theaters and campaigns you engage with.

What happens if I miss an email about a ticket release?+

Some screenings have limited seats and can move quickly. Checking your inbox during active vote periods is best.

Can I resubscribe after unsubscribing?+

Yes. Re-enter your email any time and you'll start receiving new alerts again.
